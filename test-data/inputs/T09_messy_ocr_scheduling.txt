uling

CPU scheduling decides which process in the ready
queue gets the CPU next.

FCFS (First Come First Serve) : the process that ar-
rives first is run first.  Non-preemptive.  Simple but
can cause the convoy effect - short processes wait
behind one long process.

SJF (Shortest Job First) : the process with the smal-
lest burst time runs first .  Gives the minimum aver-
age waiting time  but needs to know burst times in
advance.  Can cause starvation of long processes.
                                                      pg 15
Round Robin (RR) : each process gets a fixed time
quantum ( e.g. 4 ms ) , then goes to the back of the
ready queue .  Preemptive.  Good for time-sharing sys-
tems.  If the quantum is too large RR behaves like FCFS
; if too small , too much time is spent on context
switches .

Waiting time = Turnaround time - Burst time
Turnaround time = Completion time - Arrival time
